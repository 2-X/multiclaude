MultiClaude for Windows, version 1.0.0
======================================

Run several copies of the Claude desktop app at the same time, each with its
own sign-in, chats, settings and icon color (for example Personal and Work).
Your regular Claude app is not changed.

You need: Windows 10 or 11, and the Claude app installed (Microsoft Store or
claude.com/download).

INSTALL

1. Right-click the zip > Extract All, and open the extracted folder.
2. Double-click "Install MultiClaude".
   - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run
     anyway. (MultiClaude isn't signed by a publisher.)
3. A Settings page opens. Find CLAUDE in the list and set it to MultiClaude.
   This makes "Continue with Google" sign-in come back to the right copy.
4. MultiClaude now sits in the notification area at the bottom right (click
   the ^ arrow if you don't see it). It starts by itself when you sign in.

MAKE A COPY

Click the MultiClaude icon > New Instance... Give it a name (Work, Personal,
a client's name), pick a color, click Create. "Claude <name>" opens; sign in
with the account that copy is for. Each copy gets its own Start menu entry and
taskbar button, so you can pin it.

Copies start empty: sign in fresh in each one. Your regular Claude keeps its
own chats and sign-in.

GOOD TO KNOW

- Closing a copy's window only hides it to its tray icon. To really quit it,
  use Quit in the MultiClaude menu or in the copy's own tray icon.
- Copies don't update themselves. MultiClaude rebuilds them when your
  regular Claude updates, and Upgrade All in its menu fetches the newest
  Claude for all of them. A copy that's open switches over when you quit it.
- Alt+` (the key left of 1) switches between all your Claude windows while one
  of them is in front.
- Remove Instance... puts a copy's app in the Recycle Bin and keeps its chats
  and settings.

UNINSTALL

Double-click "Uninstall MultiClaude" in this folder. Your copies and their chats
are kept; remove the copies from the menu first if you want them gone.
